# Amendment responsibility change is available to the configured administrators

Dedicated procedure responsibility/account-note changes retain the actual any-restriction CHANGE_PROPOSALS controller entry, without an inferred widget full-edit lock.

Group entries are alternatives. Scope resolution tests the entry’s tag restriction first, otherwise agenda restriction, otherwise Motion-type restriction. A restricted entry does not pass an unrestricted (null-context) check. Applicable shipped group templates and installation administrator assignments participate as implemented.
