# Supporter

Supporter information kept by Antragsgrün.

## Information kept

- **Target** — Target document.
- **Role** — Initiator, supporter, like or dislike.
- **Identity** — Account or anonymous continuity.
- **Person information** — Name and person information.
- **Organization** — Organization information.
- **Contact** — Contact information.
- **Order** — Participation order.
- **Gender** — Gender information used by thresholds.
- **Publicity** — Disclosure settings.
- **Resolution date** — Applicable resolution date.
