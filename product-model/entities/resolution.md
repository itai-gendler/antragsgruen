# Resolution

Resolution information kept by Antragsgrün.

## Information kept

- **Text** — Resolution text.
- **Condition** — Preliminary or final condition.
- **Date** — Resolution date.
- **Deciding body** — The body that decides the resolution.
- **Numbering** — Resolution numbering.
- **Source** — Source Motion and successor relationship.
