# Merge draft

Merge draft information kept by Antragsgrün.

## Information kept

- **Text** — Saved paragraph and section text.
- **Selections** — Selected Amendments and original/proposed versions.
- **Handled warnings** — Saved acknowledgements of warnings.
- **Dispositions** — Proposed Amendment dispositions.
- **Voting information** — Saved voting information.
- **Protocol** — Saved protocol.
- **Removed sections** — Sections selected for removal.
- **Publicity** — Whether the saved draft is public.
- **Saved date** — Time the working draft was saved.
- **Deleted** — Whether the draft was consumed or deleted.
